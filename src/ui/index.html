About Audiority

Inspects each audio track, applies your receiver and playback-path profile, and uses FFmpeg to convert unsupported tracks to DTS, Dolby Digital (AC-3), or stereo PCM. Supported tracks and video are copied into a new MKV. Originals are never replaced.

Current limitations

  • DTS and AC-3 are lossy. This is a compatibility policy, not a measurement of the least quality loss.
  • Unlisted channel layouts remain unchanged and unresolved. Approved downmix rules and compatible DTS-core extraction are optional.
  • No video encoding, GPU audio acceleration, automatic device detection, or Plex/Jellyfin integration. Direct play is not guaranteed.
  • Most receiver entries require user-confirmed capabilities. TV/app names do not trigger capability lookup.
  • No DTS-HD, TrueHD or Atmos encoding. Re-encoding can discard object metadata.
  • MKV output only. Incompatible streams fail safely. Hard links are preferred; unsupported drives use an exclusive copy requiring extra space. Wait for Complete before using the output. FAT32 retains its per-file size limit.
  • One file at a time. Queue recovery is available after restart; interrupted jobs restart from the beginning. The output destination can be remembered in Settings. Disk space and decoder support still limit inputs.
  • Public packaging is blocked by the current development FFmpeg build. Windows runtime validation is outstanding.
